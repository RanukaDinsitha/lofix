# 🎧 LoFix

An online lofi music player tailored to your personal tastes. LoFix is a fork of [Lofi FM](https://github.com) that learns what you like to play the perfect background tracks while you work, study, or relax. 

It is a fully client-side application powered entirely by the YouTube Data API, designed to give you the perfect distraction-free vibes while coding.

🌐 **Try the website:** [YOUR-DEPLOYED-URL-HERE]

---

### ✨ Features

* **Taste-Based Playback** 🧠 – Plays lofi music tailored to your personal liking.
* **Custom YouTube Videos** 🎥 – Add your favourite YouTube tracks.
* **Custom Theming** 🎨 – Personalise the interface to match your style.
* **Custom Backgrounds** 🖼️ – Change the background of the player.
* **Draggable Controls** 🕹️ – Move the player controls anywhere on your screen.
* **Quick Access Starring** ⭐ – Star your top songs for instant playback.
* **Video Scaling Controls** 🔄 – Enable or disable background video scaling.
